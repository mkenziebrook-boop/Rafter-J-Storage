Rental Agreement

This Rental Agreement is entered into pursuant to Chapter 59 of the Texas Property Code, between Rafter J Properties, LLC "Lessor" and the Tenant, "Tenant", as further identified in the tenant information section of this agreement, executed at the facility's location on the date specified. In consideration of the covenants to be performed by Tenant, Lessor agrees to rent storage space to Tenant subject to the following terms and conditions:

  1. This Agreement will commence on the date executed for a term of one month and will automatically renew on a month-to-month basis until termination in accordance with the terms of this Agreement. The Lessor has the option to prorate any renewal term to the first of the following month and if prorated, the term will be month-to-month beginning on the first day of each month thereafter.
  2. Tenant expressly agrees that all covenants and conditions of this Agreement apply to any renewal, extension, or holding over.
  3. Tenant agrees to pay rent and other charges in advance and without demand at the facility's location or such other place as may be designated in writing by Lessor. All payments from Tenant to Lessor will first be applied to nonrent obligations of Tenant, then to rent. Lessor reserves the right to require the payment be made in cash, cashier's check, or other certified payment.
  4. Tenant promises to vacate the space and the premises immediately on expiration of the agreed term. If for any reason Tenant remains in the space after notice of termination is given by either party in this Agreement, Tenant will be considered a Tenant at sufferance and agrees to pay a daily rental rate equal to double the daily rate of the monthly rental. Tenant agrees that all covenants and conditions of this Agreement apply to any Tenant at sufferance.
  5. Tenant agrees that payment is not contingent on receipt of any statement or request for payment from Lessor and that, in the event Lessor sends a statement, it is sent only as a courtesy and not as an obligation.
  6. Tenant agrees to pay the amount specified as a deposit, which Lessor agrees to return to Tenant without interest after termination of this Agreement, provided Tenant has complied with all of the terms, conditions, and covenants of this Agreement. Refunds are made by check, not cash. Tenant agrees that the deposit may be commingled with Lessor's other funds, will not accrue interest, and is not an advance payment of rent or a measure of Lessor's damages in case of default by Tenant. To receive a refund of deposit, Tenant must give Lessor a written request for refund of deposit designating the correct address and telephone number of Tenant and must have fully complied with the terms of this Agreement. Deposit refunds are delivered when mailed by regular mail to the last known address designated by Tenant.
  7. If any check given to Lessor by Tenant or other person on Tenant's behalf is returned uncollected by a bank for any reason, Tenant agrees to pay a returned check charge in the amount specified.
  8. Lessor has the option of accepting partial payment of rent or charges. Acceptance of partial payment does not cure any default, void notices, or give Tenant any right to continue occupying the space unless agreed to in writing by Lessor.
  9. Tenant agrees to notify Lessor by written notice, signed and dated, within 10 days of any change of Tenant's address, telephone number, or other information that deviates from the information provided by Tenant in this Agreement. Actual notice to Lessor is required. An address printed on an envelope or check is not notice to Lessor of change of address for purposes of enforcement of Lessor's lien or other notices sent by Lessor to Tenant.
  10. Tenant may terminate this Agreement by giving Lessor written notice of intent to vacate the space 10 days before the end of any term. Tenant will forfeit the deposit on failure to give written notice of move-out.
  11. Lessor may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving Tenant 30 days' written notice of intent not to renew the rental, or by giving Tenant three days' written notice of filing for judicial eviction.
  12. On termination of this Agreement, Tenant agrees to remove his or her lock, vacate the space, leave it clean and in reasonable and rerentable condition, notify Lessor that the premises are vacant and clean, submit a request for refund, and sign a release in the form set forth by Lessor. Tenant will forfeit the deposit for failure to perform any of these obligations.
  13. Tenant agrees to provide his or her own lock and to lock the space immediately after execution of this Agreement. Tenant agrees to keep the space locked when Tenant is not on the premises. If any space is found unlocked, Lessor may overlock the space until Tenant's lock is replaced and Tenant notifies Lessor that the space has been locked by Tenant.
  14. Tenant agrees that the space will be conclusively deemed abandoned when the space is left unlocked for a period of 72 hours, and Lessor has a reasonable belief that Tenant has abandoned the space. Lessor may reasonably believe that Tenant has abandoned the space if (1) Tenant has given notice of move-out before the date on which the space was found to be unlocked; (2) Lessor did not participate in removing the lock; (3) the space was found empty or near empty; or (4) Lessor has been unable to reach Tenant through the telephone number and address provided by Tenant.

    If the space is found unlocked and Tenant has not given notice to vacate, Lessor may reasonably believe that Tenant has abandoned the space if (1) Lessor is unable to reach Tenant by calling the last known telephone number of Tenant; that is, the number listed by Tenant on this Agreement or a subsequently filed written notice; and (2) Tenant has mailed a notice of unlocked space to Tenant by regular mail.

    A reasonable belief does not require belief beyond a reasonable doubt and is a subjective standard in Lessor's opinion. Tenant requests Lessor, as agent for Tenant, to discard or otherwise dispose of any property left in or around the abandoned space and to hold Lessor harmless from liability for disposal of property. Tenant forfeits any deposit on abandonment.

  15. Tenant will pay to Lessor the cost of repairing any damage, except normal wear and tear, to the space or the facility caused by Tenant, Tenant's employees, agents, assigns, invitees, family, or servants.
  16. Tenant will use the space for storage only and will not store any property that Tenant does not have the legal right to possess. Tenant agrees to abide by Lessor's decision regarding prohibited use of the space. Tenant will not store any of the following in the space: asbestos; explosives; fireworks; ammunition; paint; gasoline; oil; fuel; grease; flammable, combustible, chemical, odorous, corrosive, pollutant, toxic, or other inherently dangerous or hazardous materials or waste; marijuana or other controlled substances; tires; batteries; weapons prohibited by law; produce; grass or debris from yard or construction activity; liquids; any living creature or organism; dead animals or carcasses; or any property or controlled substance that violates any order or regulation of the Board of Health, Fire, Police, Sanitation or other governmental body.

    The space must not be used for the following purposes or activities without permission from Lessor: parties; flea markets; garage sales; musical concerts or practice; lodging or sleeping; cooking; selling directly from the space; spraying paint; sanding; using power tools; welding; automotive repair or maintenance; building floats; or any other activity that creates a nuisance, damages the facility, or violates any local, state, or federal ordinance, zoning, code or law. Tenant will not interfere with, annoy, or disturb other Tenants and will not store or do anything that may jeopardize the health or safety of any persons or property on the premises. Tenant agrees not to bring pets onto the premises.

  17. Tenant will not alter or modify the space in any manner without express written consent of Lessor. If Tenant causes any item to become affixed to any structure of the facility, the item will automatically become the exclusive permanent property of Lessor without liability to Tenant for its cost for any reason, including without limitation, removal by Lessor.
  18. If Tenant breaches any covenant or condition of this Agreement, Tenant gives Lessor the option to terminate the Agreement immediately and Tenant agrees to vacate the space and facility within 24 hours of oral or written notice of termination by Lessor.
  19. Time is of the essence of this Agreement. The following will be deemed to be events of default by Tenant under this Agreement.
    1. Payment for rent or any charge is not received at Lessor's office when due and payable;
    2. Tenant fails or refuses to timely comply with any one or more of the terms, conditions, or covenants of this Agreement;
    3. Tenant abandons the space;
    4. Tenant makes an assignment of the space;
    5. Tenant fails to timely give notice of change of address and telephone number;
    6. Tenant give false information regarding his identity, address, telephone or business; or
    7. Tenant is the subject of legal action concerning the right to use and occupy the space.
  20. Section 59.021 of the Texas Property Code grants Lessor a lien on all property in a self-service storage facility for the payment of charges that are due and unpaid by the Tenant. Tenant grants Lessor a contractual landlord's lien and self-service storage facility lien pursuant to Chapter 59 of the Texas Property Code on all property in Tenant's space as a priority lien to secure payment of all sums due and unpaid under this Agreement.
  21. If Tenant is in default or breach of this Agreement, Lessor may choose any one or all of the following remedies without prejudice to any other remedy and without additional notice to Tenant:
    1. Deny Tenant access to the space or the facility and overlock Tenant's space;
    2. Charge a late charge, overlock charge, the reasonable expenses of foreclosure, or other charges set forth in this Agreement;
    3. Terminate this Agreement by giving Tenant three days' written notice to vacate;
    4. Exercise any remedy or right allowed by law;
    5. Enforce the lien granted to Lessor under Chapter 59 of the Texas Property Code by seizing and selling the property under a judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction that forecloses the lien and orders the sale of property to which it is attached;
    6. Charge attorney's fees, costs, and an eviction fee to compensate Lessor for time, inconvenience, and overhead for filing an eviction suit;
    7. Enforce the contractual landlord's lien granted in this Agreement by seizing and selling the property according to the procedures in Chapter 59 of the Texas Property Code. Chapter 59 of the Texas Property Code requires that:
      1. Lessor will deliver to Tenant written notice of the claim, which contains: (a) an itemized account of the claim; (b) the name, address, and telephone number of the Lessor or Lessor's Agent; (c) a statement that the contents of the self-service storage space have been seized under the contractual landlord's lien; and (d) a statement that if the claim is not satisfied before the 15th day after the day that notice is delivered, the property will be sold at public auction. Notice by mail is considered delivered when the notice, properly addressed with postage prepaid, is deposited with the United States Postal Service.
      2. If Tenant fails to satisfy the claim before the 15th day after the day that the notice is delivered in person or mailed by certified mail to Tenant's last known address, Lessor will publish or post a notice advertising the sale. The notice will include: (a) a general description of the property; (b) a statement that the property is being sold to satisfy a landlord's lien; (c) the Tenant's name; (d) the address of the facility; and (e) the time, place, and terms of the sale. Notice will be published once in each of two consecutive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation in the county where the facility is located, or if there is no newspaper of general circulation in the county, Lessor will post a copy of the notice at the facility and at least five other conspicuous locations near the facility.
      3. If notice is by publication, Lessor may not sell the property until the 15th day after the day that the first notice is published. If notice is by posting, Lessor may sell the property after the 10th day after the day the notices are posted.
      4. The property will be sold at public sale, to be held at Rafter J Properties, LLC, 36 Hunters Circle, Victoria, TX. The sale will be conducted according to the terms specified in the notice of sale. Sale will be made to the high bidder, although Lessor retains the right to reject all bids, and to withdraw items of property from the sale. Property may be sold item-by-item, in groupings, or by the entire space at Lessor's option. Sale may be by oral or written bid. Tenant gives Lessor the right to dispose of remaining property in any manner Lessor sees fit after Lessor has complied with Chapter 59 of the Texas Property Code. If no bids are received on any part or all of the stored property, Lessor may dispose of the property in any manner whatsoever, including disposal in trash cans.
      5. Lessor will deliver written notice of any excess proceeds of the sale to Tenant's last known address and deliver any excess to Tenant if requested in writing by Tenant within two years of the sale. If the excess proceeds are not claimed within two years after the date of the sale, they will become the property of Lessor.
  22. No waiver by Lessor, its agents, employees, or representatives, of any breach or default of any covenant or condition of this Agreement will constitute a waiver of any subsequent breach or default.
  23. Tenant may redeem property by paying all sums due before the time a bid is accepted by Lessor at the foreclosure sale. Lessor has no duty of bailment or safe keeping for redeemed property. Tenant agrees to remove the redeemed property immediately on redemption. If Tenant fails to remove the redeemed property immediately, Tenant will pay rent at triple the regular rate for any space plus other applicable fees set forth in this Agreement.
  24. Tenant expressly grants Lessor the right to enter the space for inspection, repair, or improvement as deemed necessary by Lessor. If Tenant fails to provide access to Lessor within 48 hours after Lessor's oral or written request for access, or if Lessor is unable to reach Tenant by telephone or regular mail after making reasonable attempts using the information provided by Tenant in this Agreement or subsequently in writing, Tenant expressly grants Lessor the right to remove Tenant's lock and enter the space for the purpose of inspection, repair, or improvement. If Lessor removes Tenant's lock, Tenant will replace the lock at no cost to Lessor. Tenant agrees that Lessor's entrance into the space does not constitute trespass, does not create a bailment, and does not alter Tenant's exclusive care, custody, and control of the stored property. Tenant agrees that Lessor will have the right to move the property stored in the space to another space as needed, in Lessor's opinion, for the purpose of conducting an inspection or making repairs or improvements, and waives any claim for any resulting damages or injury.
  25. In case of emergency, Tenant expressly grants Lessor the right to remove Tenant's lock, enter the space immediately, and take whatever steps Lessor deems appropriate for preservation of Tenant's property and/or the facility, including removal of the property to another location. An "emergency" is any situation that gives rise to a reasonable belief that a hazard or danger to persons or property exists, in Lessor's opinion. Tenant waives any claim for damages for any injury, damage, or inconvenience that may arise through actions taken for emergency, unless due to the willful misconduct or gross negligence of Lessor, and, in such event, only up to a maximum of $100.
  26. In the event of fire, property damage, break-in, or other emergency, if Tenant is unavailable or otherwise unable to participate at the facility, Tenant authorizes Lessor to contact the person listed as emergency contact to locate Tenant or to act as Tenant's authorized agent to enter the premises or remove stored property.
  27. Tenant will not plug in any electrical appliances or equipment to any electrical outlet at the facility without written permission of Lessor. Lessor will not be liable for loss or damage, resulting from failure, interruption, or malfunction of any utilities, fixtures, or equipment.
  28. Lessor's employees and agents will not perform services for Tenant. If Tenant requests services from Lessor's agents or employees, whether with or without pay, the agent or employee will be conclusively deemed the agent or employee of Tenant in the performance of the services. Tenant agrees to hold Lessor and Lessor's agents and employees harmless from any claim or cause of action arising directly or indirectly from the performance of such services.
  29. Lessor is not liable to Tenant for injury or death as a result of Tenant's use of the rented space or the facility. Lessor does not take care, custody, control, possession, or dominion over Tenant's property and does not provide protection for Tenant, the space, Tenant's property, or the facility. Lessor has no obligation or liability to Tenant, Tenant's invitees, family, employees, agents, or servants for loss of or damage to persons or property due to conduct or negligence of other Tenants or third parties; the active or passive acts or omissions or negligence of the Lessor or Lessor's agents or employees; theft; vandalism; acts of God; pests; fire; smoke; explosions; moisture; water; weather; injunction; riot; court order; or any other cause whatsoever.
  30. Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold Lessor harmless from any and all fines, levies, losses, claims, demands, causes of action, costs, including attorney's fees, or other proceedings arising directly or indirectly from this agreement or the use of the space or facility by Tenant or Tenant's agents, employees, invitees, or family, or guests. Tenant's obligations for indemnity specifically includes costs, fines, or penalties arising out of the storage or disposal of any toxic or hazardous material.
  31. Tenant will not sublet any portion of the space or assign this Agreement without advance written permission of Lessor.
  32. If during the term of this Agreement, the space is taken for any public or quasi-public use under any statute or by right of eminent domain or purchased under threat of such taking, this Agreement will terminate on the date the condemning authority or purchaser takes possession of the space. Any rent paid in advance for an unused term will be adjusted on a pro rata basis. Tenant will have no interest of any kind in any sale or condemnation proceeds.
  33. If Lessor institutes any proceeding arising from any covenant or condition of this Agreement, Tenant agrees to pay Lessor's reasonable attorney's fees, costs of court, and other expenses associated with the proceeding unless Lessor is found to have acted with malicious intent or gross-negligence. Lessor will be entitled to pre- and post-judgment interest at the highest legal rate.
  34. Lessor may establish hours of access to the facility, and special hours of access may be permitted for particular Tenants. Lessor may require identification of any person on facility property, and condition access in any manner deemed reasonable by Lessor to maintain the facility. Tenant agrees to abide by access controls established by Lessor and to carry proper identification when on the premises. Tenant acknowledges that access to the facility may vary and access controls may fail from time to time. Tenant does not rely on any visible or implied access controls for security and agrees to take full responsibility to safeguard Tenant's property regardless of whether access is controlled.
  35. Tenant agrees that the insurer of Tenant or Tenant's property located at the facility will not be subrogated to any claim that Tenant may have against Lessor, Lessor's agents or employees, and releases Lessor from all liability for all claims covered by Tenant's insurance.
  36. Tenant agrees that the aggregate value of property stored in the space will not exceed $______ (entered below).
  37. Tenant accepts this Agreement subject and subordinate to any and all mortgages, liens, leases, or deeds of trust in existence at the time of execution of this Agreement, subsequently amended, renewed or extended, or which may hereafter exist. Tenant gives Lessor power, as Tenant's agent, to subordinate this Agreement to any mortgage, lease, deed of trust, or other lien hereafter placed on any part of the facility. Tenant agrees that, in the event that the facility, or any part of the facility, is sold, including sale through foreclosure, Tenant will attorn to the purchaser. In that event, Tenant agrees to recognize the purchaser as Lessor under this Agreement and, at any time requested by the purchaser, to execute and deliver any documents that may be necessary to evidence attornment.
  38. Whenever possible, each provision of this Agreement will be interpreted in a manner so as to be effective and valid under applicable law. If any provision of this Agreement is held by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect and will in no way be affected, impaired, or invalidated.
  39. Charges are not interest, but are liquidated damages for Lessor's time, inconvenience, and overhead related to the collection of rent or other default of Tenant. If any charge is deemed to be interest, any amount in excess of the legally permitted amount will be deemed a mutual mistake and refunded to Tenant by Lessor without penalty to Lessor.
  40. This Agreement will apply to, bind, and inure to the benefit of the successors, heirs, assigns, and representatives of the parties.
  41. This Agreement will be governed and construed in accordance with the law of the State of Texas, and venue will be in Victoria County, where the facility is located.
  42. Tenant agrees to abide by all rules and regulations that are posted in a conspicuous place at the facility at the time of signing this Agreement or as amended from time to time by notice mailed to the Tenant by regular mail. Tenant agrees that the rules and regulations are incorporated into this Agreement for all purposes.
  43. Lessor may change the terms of this Agreement on 30 days' written notice mailed by regular mail to Tenant's last known address. Tenant agrees to accept changes in full or terminate the tenancy without holding over.
  44. This Agreement constitutes the sole and entire Agreement of the parties relating to the rights and the obligations assumed. No oral representations or modifications concerning this Agreement will have any effect. Lessor's agent is not authorized to waive or alter any terms, or to make representations that impose any duty on the Lessor not included in this Agreement. There are no other special conditions or provisions other than those listed below or attached and signed by Lessor and Tenant.
  45. Tenant stores property at Tenant's own risk. Tenant agrees to purchase and maintain insurance coverage, at Tenant's expense, for 100 percent of the replacement cost of Tenant's stored property for the benefit of both Lessor and Tenant. Failure to carry the required insurance is a breach of this Agreement and Tenant assumes all risk of loss that would be covered by such insurance.
  46. If Tenant is in default or breaches any of the covenants or conditions of this Agreement, Tenant expressly waives his or her right of access to the premises and grants Lessor the right to over lock Tenant's space without removing Tenant's lock and without taking exclusive care, custody, and control of Tenant's property. Over lock does not create a bailment. Tenant agrees to pay an over lock charge.
  47. No express or implied warranties are given by Lessor. Tenant expressly waives any implied warranty of suitability, merchantability, security, or fitness for a particular purpose. Lessor's agents and employees are not authorized to make warranties or alter this Agreement except in a writing signed by the Lessor. Oral statements are not warranties and are not incorporated into this Agreement. Tenant declares that he or she has not relied on any oral statements in deciding to enter into this Agreement. Tenant acknowledges that he or she has inspected the space and facility and determined by that inspection that the space and facility are suitable for his or her purposes, as is and with all faults. Tenant agrees that Lessor does not represent or guarantee the safety or security of the property stored and has no duty of safety or security of any kind under any circumstances.
  48. Tenant agrees that Tenant will attempt to settle any dispute with Lessor, including any claim for bodily injury or property damage, by mediation before filing a lawsuit in any court.
